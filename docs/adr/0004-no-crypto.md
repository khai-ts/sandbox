# 0004: No crypto

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-09

## Decision
"Decentralized" means cooperative ownership, open source, and federated city nodes on an open protocol.
No tokens, smart contracts or crypto payments.

## Why
Riders, shops and customers in the Klang Valley already use DuitNow, FPX and e-wallets. Crypto adds
regulatory exposure (Securities Commission Malaysia) and friction without solving disputes, fraud
or insurance.
