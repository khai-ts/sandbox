# 0003: Payments in Malaysia

- **Status:** Proposed (needs quotes and a decision)
- **Date:** 2026-10-09

## Context
The co-op must never hold customer funds itself; otherwise it may need Bank Negara Malaysia
approval. We need a licensed payment provider with **marketplace split payouts** to shops and riders,
and cheap local rails: DuitNow QR, FPX, cards, e-wallets (Touch 'n Go, GrabPay, Boost).

## Options to evaluate
Stripe (Connect availability for Malaysian connected accounts), Curlec by Razorpay, Billplz,
Fiuu (Razer Merchant Services), iPay88. Compare: split/escrow support, payout speed to riders,
DuitNow QR and FPX pricing, onboarding (KYC) friction, API quality.

## Decision
Pending. Nudge customers toward the cheapest rail (likely DuitNow QR or FPX), and show the cost
difference at checkout.

## Out of scope
No crypto or stablecoins (founder decision, 2026-10-09).
