# Fairplate (working name)

A people-owned, low-fee food delivery network, piloting in the **Klang Valley, Malaysia**:
open source, federated by city, and governed as a cooperative (koperasi) of riders, shops and customers.

- **Shops** pay 0% commission.
- **Riders** keep 100% of delivery fees and tips (less statutory SOCSO) and set their own minimums.
- **Customers** pay a flat RM0.99 network fee, with payment costs passed through at cost.
- **Software** is maintained mostly by Claude Code under human governance.
